Research & valuation · September 2026

Five markets are converging on one gap. The portfolio is built in the gap. Market research, a valuation ladder keyed to evidence, and strategy for twenty-two agent-infrastructure domains.

The agent economy in 2026 has a peculiar shape: capital has never been more concentrated, adoption has never been wider, and almost nobody can say afterwards what their agent actually did. This page researches the markets the [&] portfolio sits inside, prices its domains with a method you can disagree with line by line, and says which of its bets the market is about to reward, which it is about to commoditise, and what should be sold first.

01 · The thesis in one diagram

Everyone is shipping agents. Almost no one can replay one.

The five markets below are usually analysed separately. Put them side by side and they point at the same missing thing: a record of what an agent was allowed to do, what it did, and whether the two agree, that a second machine can check.

The operationalisation gap

Gartner's 40% cancellation forecast, a 17% replay rate among organisations running agents, and prompt injection in a third of sampled skills are the same finding three times: the agent stack can produce actions faster than it can account for them. The [&] portfolio's product is the accounting — composition that refuses instead of degrading, receipts a stranger can re-derive, and a world that is a graph rather than a snapshot.

That is a narrow claim and it is meant to be. The portfolio does not compete on model quality, on hosting, or on retrieval accuracy, and this page will show that it would lose on all three. It competes on the part of the stack that the compliance calendar, the security lists and the cancellation statistics are all now pointing at, and it competes there with running code whose claims were derived rather than typed.

02 · Five markets, sized by named sources

The numbers, and which layer of the portfolio each one feeds.

Analyst forecasts for “agentic AI” vary by a factor of ten depending on what is counted. Ranges are shown rather than a point, and the definition each firm uses is named where it changes the answer.

Market 1

Agentic AI

$10.9–12B → ~$53B 2026 → 2030 · 44–46% CAGR

MarketsandMarkets' AI-agents series is the most-quoted band. Its narrower “enterprise agentic AI” cut runs $5.3B to $24.5B over the same years. Grand View Research's wider definition reaches $183B by 2033. Gartner expects 40% of enterprise applications to embed task-specific agents by end of 2026, from under 5% in 2025.

Feeds everything — this is the demand side. The portfolio's exposure is the governance and evidence slice of it, not the whole.

Agent memory

$48M+ direct venture funding into five named competitors

Mem0 $24M (Series A, Oct 2025), Letta $10M, Cognee $7.5M, Hindsight $3.6M, Zep $3.3M. Retrieval quality has converged: Mem0 94.4%, Mastra 94.87%, OMEGA 95.4% on LongMemEval with cloud readers. The category's next fight is provenance and cost per recall, not accuracy.

Feeds Graphonomous, and warns it: the headline benchmark is no longer where the margin is. See §5.

Market 3

Governance, audit and compliance

Art. 12 enforceable for high-risk systems since 2 August 2026

The EU AI Act now requires automatic, lifetime event logging for high-risk AI, retained at least six months on both provider and deployer sides, with penalties to 3% of turnover. OWASP's Agentic Top 10 (Dec 2025) names memory poisoning, tool misuse and rogue agents. A Q1 2026 survey of 420 organisations found only 17% could reconstruct an agent's tool-call sequence after the fact.

Feeds box-and-box, Delegatic, Workbench's proof gates, TRAAVIIS receipts. This is the market where “a certificate on every verdict” stops being a slogan.
EU AI Act text via Help Net Security · OWASP GenAI project · trade survey
Market 4

Local and sovereign compute

on-device inference share rising; sovereignty a board topic in 2026

Trade coverage puts enterprise inference at the edge or on-premises well above its 2023 level, and “sovereign AI” has moved from a compliance niche to national infrastructure budgets. Small models now do real reasoning in a few billion parameters on hardware people already own. The Cloudflare Agents SDK, by contrast, makes a hosted stateful agent cost nothing when idle.

Feeds T&R, Super (CD), [World] Cloud — an operating system that ships its own verifier is a sovereignty product before it is anything else. Tension: Cloudflare is both the portfolio's cloud and a competitor's hosting.
trade press (labelled) · Cloudflare docs
Market 5

World models

$3B+ raised by world-model startups in H1 2026

AMI Labs (~$1B), World Labs ($1B), General Intuition ($320M), Odyssey ($310M), Decart ($300M). Their “world” is a learned simulator of physics and causality that agents train inside. It is not this portfolio's “world”, which is a machine, its state and the record of how it got that way, in three named sizes.

Feeds nothing directly, and threatens the vocabulary. Every ComputeDriven page must keep saying which “world” it means. Opportunity: a learned world model is itself a thing whose runs need receipts.

03 · The standards layer settled this year

One foundation now holds both protocols. That changes what a composition layer has to prove.

In December 2025 Anthropic, Block and OpenAI formed the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation and donated MCP, goose and AGENTS.md to it, with Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare and Bloomberg backing. On 17 August 2026 Google's A2A joined the same foundation. Agent-to-tool and agent-to-agent are now governed in one place, with an enterprise roadmap that prioritises audit trails, SSO and retry semantics.

MCP's own numbers: more than 10,000 published servers and around 97 million monthly SDK downloads. The skills layer above it grew from one registry to eight marketplaces in six months and now indexes somewhere between 800,000 and 1.9 million SKILL.md files depending on who is counting. Quality is the story there: an audit of 22,511 skills found 140,963 issues, and Snyk found prompt injection in 36% of a sample.

The [&] Protocol's position is that it sits above both: it composes capabilities and compiles the result to MCP configurations and A2A Agent Cards. That claim got cheaper to make and easier to test this year, because the targets stopped moving. It also got more necessary, because the thing the foundation is standardising is transport, and the thing the skills audits are finding is that nothing at the transport layer refuses a bad part.

04 · Capital, and what it is not funding

A record year that mostly went to two companies.

Global venture funding reached about $510 billion in the first half of 2026, more than all of 2025, with AI taking over 70% of it (roughly 80% in Q1). OpenAI and Anthropic alone absorbed $217 billion, 43% of every startup dollar in the half. At seed, Carta's Q1 median was $3.2M raised on a $24M post-money, with AI companies carrying roughly a 42% premium over non-AI peers.

Underneath that, the shape is a barbell. Solo founders were 63% of new startups in 2026 by one payments-processor count, and “one-person company” is a normal thing to say about an infrastructure business. But fewer than 5% of the 12,000-odd public MCP servers had monetised by March 2026, and those that had mostly cleared $500 to $3,000 a month.

Two readings follow for this portfolio. First, its comparables have been funded at the paper stage: Letta at a research prototype, Mem0 before its graph tier. A working kernel with derived laws, an operating system image and a memory server on npm is more than several of those pitches carried. Second, that observation has been true since May and has changed nothing, because a term sheet is written against traction, not against a law count. Which returns to the review's finding: the missing input is one outsider, not more code.

The uncomfortable measurement

The status file records that topology on versus off on this benchmark is +0.3 points, and that no flat-RAG baseline was run. So the thing Graphonomous is for has not yet been shown to earn its keep where it is easiest to measure. The honest research position is that LongMemEval does not exercise cyclic knowledge, and the honest engineering position is that a benchmark which does needs to be published before the invariant is a selling point rather than a proof.

Where the category is going: the December 2025 survey “Memory in the Age of AI Agents” calls memory a first-class primitive; the 2026 papers that matter (NeSyC, trainable graph memory, ALMA) put a symbolic, checkable layer over the neural one; the May 2026 “replay divergence” preprint names the failure the compliance market is now paying for. All three point at provenance and replay, which is where the portfolio's instruments are, and away from the accuracy race, which is where its marketing was.

06 · A valuation ladder keyed to evidence rungs

Price the rung, not the story.

The May page put the portfolio at $1.04M “realistic” on domains alone. That number had no method a reader could reject. This one does: the portfolio is worth what its highest occupied rung makes sellable, and each rung has comparables.

domains
$60K–$180K
Bare aftermarket value of 22 mostly two-word .com/.org names with no traffic. Comparables: two-word AI-adjacent .coms clear low four to low five figures; the six-figure market is single words and short acronyms.
+ specs & docs
$150K–$400K
Fourteen 400–1,150 line specs, a 434-URL docs atlas, five masterclasses. Priced as acquirable content and positioning, roughly what an acqui-hire would attribute to “they already wrote it down”.
+ derived code
$0.6M–$1.5M
A kernel with 210 derived laws, an OS image with its verifier, a memory server on npm, a recorder on real data, four products on Fly. Pre-seed IP comparables: Letta was funded at a paper; Higher Order at a prototype. Discounted because nothing here has been consumed.
+ one external
$1.5M–$4M
First outside reproduction, first storage byte from a stranger, first B1 run. Crosses into the pre-seed band Carta reports for AI ($17.9M median pre-money is the top of the range, not the expectation).
+ revenue
$4M–$25M
A storage or certification SKU with paying users and a receipt they re-derived. AI-infrastructure multiples of 20× revenue are quoted in the trade; a bootstrapped audit-tooling company with $50K MRR sits at the bottom of this band.

Bands are estimates by the method stated, not appraisals, and they widen to the right on purpose. The portfolio occupies the middle rung today. The step from the middle rung to the next one is the cheapest in the ladder and the only one that has not been attempted.

Why the bare-domain band is a fifth of May's

The 2026 aftermarket rewards single words (ai.com $70M, club.com $10M) and one-word .ai names (bot.ai $1.2M, neural.ai $1.75M). Compound coinages such as agentromatic or deliberatic are brands, not keywords; they are worth what stands on them, which is exactly why the ladder above prices the standing rather than the name. The two exceptions are computedriven.com and webhost.systems, which read as generic categories, and fleetprompt.com and specprompt.com, which carry a live keyword.

08 · Strategy

What the market will commoditise, what it will not, and what to sell first.

Will be commoditised by mid-2027 · do not compete here

Retrieval, hosting, chat

  • Memory accuracy. Five funded systems within two points of each other; the ceiling is the reader model, which none of them own.
  • Agent hosting. Cloudflare gives every agent a Durable Object with state, hibernation, scheduling and MCP for free when idle. WebHost.Systems' original pitch is now a product feature of the portfolio's own cloud provider.
  • Agent builders. Agentelic's category has hundreds of entrants and Gartner counts ~130 real ones. The [&] integration is the only durable reason to keep it.
  • The word “world”. Three billion dollars just bought it for a different meaning.
Will compound · this is the portfolio

Refusal, receipts, re-derivation

  • Composition that refuses. Two parts built without knowledge of each other either compose with a certificate or return zero with a reason. No transport standard does this and the skills audits show why it is needed.
  • Receipts a stranger can check. Article 12 wants automatic, lifetime, replayable records of what an agent did. The portfolio's entire method is that.
  • An OS that carries its verifier. Sovereignty budgets are buying exactly the property T&R was built around: don't trust the number, re-run it.
  • Derived counts. Boring, and the reason the rest can be believed. The portfolio has corrected its own law count six times in public; its competitors' benchmark tables have not been corrected once.

Sell in this order

  1. Workbench, to people already using Claude Code. The raw material is on 39% of professional developers' disks. A session that records itself into a signed, scored bundle is a compliance artifact and a skill at once. It needs hooks, not marketing.
  2. Storage, through [World] Cloud. Sync and restore before hosted compute (ruled), entitlement before checkout (ruled), roughly $1.35 per 100 GB per month at cost with zero egress. The first byte from a stranger is the first revenue datapoint and the first external rung simultaneously.
  3. Certification, through FleetPrompt. A marketplace that installs only what carries a certificate is the answer to a statistic (36% of sampled skills carry prompt injection) that a buyer already believes. Reposition it from “prior design” to the install gate.
  4. Delegatic, when its store leaves memory. Policy-as-code for agents is an existing category (OPA-shaped) with a compliance calendar behind it.

Park, on the record

AgenTroMatic, Deliberatic, GeoFleetic and TickTickClock are complete specs with no code and no near-term consumer. Parking them by name in the status file costs nothing, removes four rows from the scope grade in the review, and keeps the names, which are worth more than the specs. Un-park any of them the day a shipped product needs the primitive.

The one experiment

Everything on this page prices higher one rung to the right, and the step to that rung is the cheapest in the ladder: hand one outsider the docs atlas and one task, or get one stranger to reproduce one receipt. It has been runnable since 2026-08-11. Until it runs, the market research above describes a market the portfolio is beside, not in.
